About me

I am at the School of Management and Engineering, Nanjing University. My research interests include large language models for operations research (LLM4OR), data-driven decision-making, and large language models for machine learning (LLM4ML).

My recent work explores how multi-agent systems can support optimization modeling, model selection under uncertainty, and learning-based prediction.
